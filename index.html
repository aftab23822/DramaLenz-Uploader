DramaLenz Uploader

A private publishing tool used only by the owner of the DramaLenz channel.

What it does

DramaLenz publishes Urdu discussion videos about Pakistani dramas. The DramaLenz Uploader is the channel owner's own automation. It takes the owner's finished videos and short vertical clips and uploads them to the owner's own DramaLenz YouTube channel and DramaLenz Facebook Page, with their titles, descriptions and planned publish times.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other people's accounts. It runs in the owner's private, automated build environment.

What it accesses

  • YouTube Data API: upload videos to the owner's channel, read the status of those uploads, and remove the tool's own unpublished (private) drafts when the owner asks to redo them.
  • Facebook Pages API: publish and schedule reels on the owner's own Page.
