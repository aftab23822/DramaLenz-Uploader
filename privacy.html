Privacy Policy

DramaLenz Uploader · last updated 22 November 2026

Who this covers

The DramaLenz Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the DramaLenz YouTube channel and Facebook Page. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data handled by YouTube and Google.

What data the tool accesses

  • With the owner's Google sign-in (OAuth), the tool uploads the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, reads the list and status of the channel's own uploads (to confirm an upload finished and to avoid uploading the same video twice), and deletes only the private drafts the tool itself uploaded, when the owner asks to redo them.
  • With the owner's Facebook Page token, the tool uploads and schedules reels on the owner's own Page and reads their publishing status.

The tool does not access any other person's account, comments, viewers, analytics or personal information.

What data the tool stores

For each uploaded video the tool keeps a small record in the owner's private repository: the video's reference code, the platform's video id, its state (for example private, scheduled or published) and its planned publish time. The sign-in credentials are kept encrypted as secrets of the owner's private build environment and are never written to code, logs or these records. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

How long data is kept

Records are kept while the related video exists on the channel and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access tokens are short-lived and are not stored.

How to revoke access

Google access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and Facebook access in Facebook Settings → Business integrations. After revoking, the tool can no longer upload or read anything.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner through the DramaLenz YouTube channel's About page.
